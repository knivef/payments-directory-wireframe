Ad policy

The directory only works if merchants trust the rankings. These rules are public and apply to every advertiser.

  • Every ad carries a visible Sponsored label and sits in its own bordered container, separate from organic content.
  • Ad links use rel="sponsored nofollow" and open the advertiser site directly. No tracking pixels in the wireframe.
  • Ads never change ranking, verdicts, ratings or the default sort. The How we rank page says so.
  • No ads inside comparison tables, search results ordering, forms or the match tool.
  • Conversion pages are ad-free: match, assist, for providers, advertise.
  • We only accept advertisers that hold the licences their product requires. Unlicensed or prohibited services are declined.
  • Every slot reserves its height before load so ads never shift the layout.
  • Verified profiles on the paid provider tier can remove competitor sidebar ads from their own profile.
