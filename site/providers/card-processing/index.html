Card processing FAQ

What does a card processing provider do?

It connects your checkout to the card networks, authorises transactions and settles funds to your account, either directly or through an acquiring bank.

How do I compare card processing fees?

Ask every provider for the same pricing model, usually interchange plus or blended, and compare on your real ticket size and card mix rather than headline rates.
